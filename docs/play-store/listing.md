**Full description** (4,000 max)

```
Galaxy Bridge brings your Samsung Galaxy (or any Android 13+ phone) to your Mac, over Wi-Fi or a USB cable. It works together with the Galaxy Bridge app for macOS, which lives in the menu bar.

CALLS
• See who is calling on the Mac, with the contact's name and photo, and answer or decline from there
• Place calls from the Mac's menu, or by clicking any phone number on the Mac
• Find contacts as you type and see your recent calls
• The call audio stays on the phone or on the earbuds connected to it

NOTIFICATIONS AND MEDIA
• Your phone's notifications on the Mac, with pictures, quick replies and buttons
• Choose which apps show on the Mac
• See and control what is playing on the phone from the menu bar

PHOTOS AND FILES
• Browse every photo and video on the phone from the Mac
• Browse the phone's folders and send files both ways
• Take a photo with the phone and have it ready on the Mac

SCREEN MIRRORING
• The phone's screen in a window on the Mac, with sound
• Use it with the Mac's mouse, trackpad and keyboard, with copy and paste
• Android asks you to confirm every time the screen is mirrored

STATUS
• Battery, signal, storage and Do Not Disturb in the Mac's menu bar
• Battery of your Galaxy Buds and other Bluetooth accessories
• Ring the phone from the Mac to find it

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your data goes only to the Mac you pair, over your own network or the USB cable — never to the internet or to the developer
• Encrypted connection (TLS 1.3) with both devices checking each other's identity
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics
• Pair once with a QR code; after that the phone reconnects on its own

Each feature asks for its own permission, and you choose what to turn on. Screen mirroring control uses an accessibility service only to perform on the phone the taps, gestures and text you make on the Mac.

Requires the Galaxy Bridge app for macOS (macOS 14 or later).

Galaxy Bridge is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Samsung. Samsung and Galaxy are trademarks of Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd.
```
